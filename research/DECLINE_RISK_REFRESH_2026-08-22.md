## General decline-risk method reminder
For RB: age curve, NFL seasons, career touches, prior-season touches, efficiency trend, injury type/recurrence, current participation, role competition/team incentives.
For WR/TE: age, route/target efficiency trend, lower-body injury recurrence, target competition, role/snap erosion.
For QB: age/mobility decline, rushing-volume erosion, protection/injury context and offensive role.

Separate three concepts:
1. expected median decline;
2. catastrophic/availability tail risk;
3. market price already compensating for risk.
Only residual risk not adequately reflected in canonical quality/ADP should alter decision utility.

## Anti-overfit
- current camp absence alone is not a bust label;
- analyst 'risky' lists are leads/context, not numeric penalties;
- use primary participation/injury reporting where available;
- recheck immediately before draft;
- compare risk-adjusted value to alternatives at the actual pick, not in isolation.
