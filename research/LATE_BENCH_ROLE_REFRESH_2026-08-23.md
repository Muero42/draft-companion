# Late-bench role refresh — 2026-08-23

## Jacksonville RB committee: Tuten / Chris Rodriguez
Fresh Jaguars primary-source evidence strengthens the existing committee interpretation rather than resolving it to a single lead back.
- Jaguars' first unofficial 2026 depth chart lists **Bhayshul Tuten and Chris Rodriguez Jr. as co-starters** at running back.
- Aug. 13: Liam Coen said Rodriguez was getting better and better after his offseason foot issue; Rodriguez worked extensively with the first team and showed the downhill/after-contact profile the staff wants.
- Aug. 18: offensive coordinator Grant Udinski explicitly described the run game and passing-down usage as by committee; team coverage nevertheless described Tuten as a key emerging player with a big opportunity.
- Aug. 18 practice coverage again highlighted Rodriguez's inside/hidden-yardage runs.

Draft implication: neither should be treated as a locked singular RB1. Tuten retains higher explosive/asymmetric ceiling; Rodriguez has stronger evidence than a generic cheap backup because co-starter/first-team/physical-role evidence is explicit. Both are price-dependent managed-redraft upside candidates, and a large price gap can matter more than an unsupported hierarchy assumption.

## Denzel Boston
Browns primary-source camp reporting provides a stronger role signal than a generic rookie sleeper label.
- On Aug. 10 Todd Monken said Boston would work with the first-team offense because he had repeatedly made plays.
- Boston, KC Concepcion and Jerry Jeudy were the first three receivers in first-team three-WR sets that day, with Isaiah Bond rotating in.
- Boston caught multiple Shedeur Sanders passes in 11-on-11 during that practice; Browns coverage subsequently highlighted a standout Day 12 and continues to feature him in camp/preseason material.

Draft implication: Boston remains an evidence-backed late-round watch because the first-team pathway is concrete. Do not promote him solely from camp buzz; next evidence priority is whether the first-team/route participation persists through the final preseason week and how his acquisition cost moves.

No production ranking coefficient is changed by this note.
